# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Now available for Minecraft 1.21–1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1–26.1.2 and 26.2 (plus 26.3).
- On small windows or a large GUI scale the notices move above the hotbar instead of overlapping it.

## 1.0.0

First release (Minecraft 26.3).

- Bottom-right notification with a sound when the inventory becomes full.
- Low-durability warning for the item in the main hand and offhand.
- Vanilla tooltip-styled notices that slide in and stack.
- Configurable sounds (any sound id), volume, pitch, threshold and duration.
- Mod Menu + Cloth Config settings screen (optional).
- `/invfull test full|durability` and `/invfull reload` client commands.
- English and Russian translations.
